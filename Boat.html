Logbook

ENGG200 Design class at Ucalgayr final project.

I am curretnly working on the end effector(gripper) system, to detect pen location within a deadzone, then initiatign a sequence to grab the pen. I have encountered several challenges, including random interferences, proper code tweaks to ensoure accuracy, and even the continual pinching of my finger in the claw. I have decided to rework the mechanism to work with an sg90 rather than a ms4 to conserve weight and reduce the torque on the rest of the armm.

Progress Updates.

- Custom gears i built do not function with enough prescision, the gap in between gears leads to a delay when switching direction. - Currently working on rotation mechanism, have tried: bolts (best option so far), 3d pinted "clips" (too weak), I will look into pruchasing proper shafts. - ToF sesnor (VL53Ox) was detecting claw movement and intefering wiht distance readings. I have adjusted the angel of sensor range.
